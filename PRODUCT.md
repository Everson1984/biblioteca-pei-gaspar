# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the school coordination team, working during the school day to register, review, and follow up on student pedagogical and disciplinary occurrences. School leadership is a secondary audience that needs visibility into patterns and decisions.

## Product Purpose

Biblioteca Viva centralizes student and teacher records, occurrence registration, and follow-up feedback so the coordination team can act quickly and keep a consistent pedagogical history. Success means replacing scattered notes with a clear, auditable workflow.

## Positioning

The product treats discipline as a pedagogical follow-up workflow: every occurrence is connected to a student, an accountable educator, a status, and a next action rather than being only an isolated incident log.

## Operating Context

The application is used by coordination and direction on a desktop web dashboard. Staff need quick access to today's open cases, a structured occurrence form with common alternatives, and a feedback space to record conversations and agreements.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Register students and teachers.
- Register occurrences using predefined alternatives plus a free-text description.
- Track occurrence status and next steps.
- Record feedback from coordination and direction.
- The first implementation should be usable with representative local data; persistence and authentication are not established yet.
- Primary terminology: ocorrência, aluno, professor, coordenação, direção, feedback, acompanhamento.

## Product Principles

- Make the next responsible action visible.
- Keep records factual, contextual, and pedagogical.
- Reduce repetitive typing with thoughtful defaults.
- Give coordination a calm, scannable command center.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Portuguese labels, strong contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, and status meaning that is not communicated by color alone.
